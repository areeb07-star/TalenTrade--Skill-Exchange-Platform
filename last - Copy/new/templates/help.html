💡 Need Help?

We’ve got you covered! Here are some quick guides and FAQs to help you use TalenTrade effectively.

🔑 Getting Started

  • Create an account or login to your profile.
  • Add the skills you can teach and the ones you want to learn.
  • Go to Find Matches to connect with others.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I update my skills?
    Go to your Profile and click Edit Skills.
  • Why is my logo not showing?
    Make sure the logo file is in the /static folder and correctly linked in base.html.
  • How can I message my match?
    Use the Message button on the Match page to connect.
